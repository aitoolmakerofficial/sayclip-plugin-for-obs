Trademarks and Branding
=======================

The SayClip name, logo, icon, and official branding identify the original
project by AI Toolmaker (AI Toolmaker Official).

You may fork, modify, and redistribute the SayClip source code under the
project license, but you may not use the SayClip name, logo, icon, or official
branding in a way that suggests your fork, build, website, package, or
distribution is the official SayClip project.

Unofficial versions should use a clearly different name and should clearly
state that they are modified or unofficial.

Acceptable wording:

- "Fork of SayClip"
- "Based on SayClip"
- "Unofficial modified version of SayClip"

Unacceptable wording:

- "Official SayClip"
- "SayClip Pro"
- "SayClip Installer"
- "SayClip Update"
- Any wording that may confuse users into thinking the build is released or
  endorsed by AI Toolmaker (AI Toolmaker Official)
